# Who is tracked

Two settings decide this, and they do different jobs:

- **Leave out of tracking** decides whether a visitor gets Google's tag at all. A visitor with a checked role gets no tag, and nothing about their visit is sent. Use this to leave logged-in Editors untracked, for example.
- **Consent** decides what the tag may do for everyone else. A visitor who hasn't agreed to analytics, or has withdrawn, still gets the tag, but it sets no analytics cookies and sends Google only cookieless pings. See [Consent](consent.md).

Neither overrides the other. The roles you can leave out are listed in the [settings reference](../reference/settings.md#leave-out-of-tracking).
